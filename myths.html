Myth Busting

Let's clear up misconceptions about Didelphis virginiana.

Myth: Opossums are aggressive.
Fact: They prefer to flee, hiss, or play dead.
Myth: They are rodents.
Fact: They are marsupials, not related to rats.
Myth: Opossums are dirty and unhygienic
Fact: They frequently groom themselves much like cats.
Myth: Opossums can hang by their tails like monkeys.
Fact: While their tails are prehensile they are not strong enough to support their full body weight for long periods of time. Juveniles may briefly hang, but not adults.
Myth: Opossums spread diseases.
Fact: Their body temperature is actually too cold to carry rabies. They also help prevent diseases by eating pests that actually do carry diseases, such as ticks.
