What Opossums Eat

Opossums are omnivores and scavengers. They eat:

  • Insects and snails
  • Fruits and berries
  • Small rodents
  • Eggs and pet food
  • Venomous snakes
  • Carrion (tidying up roadsides)

Their diet makes them excellent natural pest control.

Oppossum Behavior

Nocturnal Lifestyle
Opossums are active at night. They usually forage for food using their keen sense of smell.
Defense Mechanisms
When seriously threatened, an opossum may go limp, drool, and lie still. This is an involuntary catatonic state, not an act. It is most commonly known as “playing dead” or “playing possum”.
Intelligence
Many studies show that opossums excel at remembering past food locations. They also have been shown to be better than domestic animals at navigating mazes.

Found One? What To Do

  1. Stay Calm and keep pets indoors.
  2. Give the animal space to leave on it's own.
  3. Call a local wildlife rescue if it is injured.
