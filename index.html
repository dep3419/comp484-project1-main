Meet the Virginia Opossum

The Virginia opossum is the only marsupial native to North America and is one of the continent's most adaptable wildlife species. They have a gentle nature, remarkable survival skills, and it's iconic “playing possum” behavior. This animal has become a familiar visitor in both rural and urban environments. This website hopes to introduce you to this perfect creature, so you can properly appreciate them.
